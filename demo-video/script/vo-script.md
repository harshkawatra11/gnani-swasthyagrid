# SwasthyaGrid demo, voiceover script

## Recording guide

- Quiet room, soft furnishings around you (curtains, a couch, a rug) to cut echo. Phone or USB mic 15 to 20 cm from your mouth, slightly off to the side, not straight on.
- Record at 48 kHz WAV if your recorder allows it. A phone voice memo (.m4a) is also fine, the pipeline converts it.
- One take per line below, saved as the exact filename shown, into `demo-video/vo/raw/`. Leave about half a second of silence at the start and end of each file, do not talk over the very first or last word.
- If a take runs longer than the "Max" time next to it, take a breath and redo it a touch faster. Do not rush the words themselves, just tighten the pauses between sentences.
- Pronunciation: SwasthyaGrid is "SWUS-thyuh grid". Gnani is "GYAH-nee".
- Tone: warm and assured, like you are walking a colleague through something you are proud of, not reading an ad. Let yourself smile a little on "Built so that Friday never comes" and on "Live, no refresh".
- Read each line two or three times if you can. More takes give the edit room to pick the best one.
